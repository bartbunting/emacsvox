6.4.2 Guided Change This Feedback ¶

  1. Move to a representative item, press C-e H, then c. This captures Current item. For feedback that has already happened, use Home’s H, select a Recent Feedback record, and press C. A record containing several parts expands numbered parts in playback order. Move with n and p to hear their text in the captured voices; O replays the part at point. Press RET to select it. Truncated history cannot supply a complete original/proposed comparison.
  2. Press c to expand component changes in the panel. Move to a choice and press RET. Content voice expands voice choices and identifies the original voice and proposed replacement. The original named voice comes first when available, followed by alternatives in alphabetical order. Press RET on a voice to select it for the draft; the panel stays open for comparison. Available changes include the content voice, adding a sound or tone, adding a spoken label, replacing an existing sound, changing a tone, or suppressing one component. You can add a cue when the original has none. The choices use the facts and components supplied by the integration; they cannot add missing semantic distinctions. RET on Parts reopens the parts; unfinished choices are retained separately when you switch between parts. Press q to go back one level: from a voice choice to Content voice, then to Change. The section closes and focus returns to its parent, whose name and state are presented through the configured cues and optional spoken labels. At the top level, q leaves the panel and preserves the unfinished draft.
  3. Press P to hear the proposed change before choosing how long to keep it. O plays the recorded original example; S stops. Proposed preview resolves the selected example with current rules and its captured buffer context. It does not apply your draft to live feedback.
  4. Press m or RET on What should match to expand the matching criteria. Each fact, state, event, and occasion shows whether it is included. Press RET on a criterion to exclude or include it; excluded criteria stay available to restore. For example, exclude state unread while keeping role field, field kind authors, and module notmuch to match authors in both read and unread messages. Keep at least one criterion. The parent row shows the resulting match; m collapses the criteria again. These are rules for every matching item, not an edit to one object.
  5. Press l for How long: the source buffer’s lifetime, this Emacs session, or a saved personal rule. A buffer rule affects every matching item in that buffer. Review the spoken summary of target, change, matching criteria, and lifetime.
  6. Press a or C-c C-c to apply or save. e instead opens the prepared rule in the Advanced editor. At the top level, q hides the guided draft; Home’s Resume unfinished changes brings it back. Aborting a choice or leaving before Apply does not change live rules. Failed saves retain the draft and report failure.

Replacing or suppressing a sound targets its identity in its before- or after-content phase, retaining other components. Existing rule data can insert only at the beginning or end of a phase; replacing a middle component therefore asks which edge to use and includes that placement in the summary. Ambiguous components require the Advanced editor. Current-item changes recheck their captured source before applying; a changed or killed target must be inspected again. Historical changes remain tied to the selected recorded example.
