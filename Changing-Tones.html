6.8.3 Change A Blank-Line Tone ¶

This changes a tone already present in recorded blank-line feedback. The available actions depend on what your integration actually produced.

  1. Navigate onto a blank line, then open C-e H H. Select that feedback record and press C. Select its part if the record has several.
  2. Press c and choose Change a tone. Select the tone, choose Pitch and duration, then enter 500 Hertz and 80 milliseconds as a trial. If the tone was between other components, choose Before the other feedback when asked about replacement placement. The summary includes this movement; inspect it along with the pitch and duration.
  3. Compare O and P. The changed example should retain its other content while changing that tone. A named tone can be selected instead of entering numbers.
  4. Press m to review the matching facts. Press l and choose This Emacs session, review the summary, and press a. Navigate onto another matching blank line to hear the live result.
  5. Restore the earlier behavior by disabling or deleting this session rule in Your feedback rules. It also expires when Emacs exits.

If the record contains a sampled cue instead of a tone, Change a tone is absent. Explain the record to identify the cue. You can suppress it with one rule and add a tone with another; remove both rules to restore the original. If no record was retained, check the history limit and repeat the action. The interface cannot infer a missing component from a visually blank line.
