6.4.5 Try A Presentation Option ¶

Presentation Options are maintained additions such as richer heading feedback or integration-specific cues. From Aural Home, open Presentation Options:

  1. Use TAB or RET on a collection to expand it.
  2. Move to an option and press P to preview it composed with the current configuration. Press C-u P to preview it in isolation.
  3. Press t to enable or disable it. Press a to hear enabled options in their exact weakest-to-strongest order.
  4. Return to the source and test the original task. Personal, session, and buffer overrides remain stronger than an enabled option.

Avoid reordering options merely to solve one exceptional object. A narrow override usually expresses that intention more clearly.
