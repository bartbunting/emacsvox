15.216.1.4 emacsvox-sudoku-board-sub-squares-summarize ¶

15.216.1.5 emacsvox-sudoku-board-summarizer ¶

Command: emacsvox-sudoku-board-summarizer ¶
Dispatch to  appropriate summarizer.

d   Number Distribution
r   Row Distribution
c   Column Distribution
s   Sub-square Distribution.
