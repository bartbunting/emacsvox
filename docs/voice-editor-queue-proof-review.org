* Why the previous rule was insufficient

Omnivox consumes a byte stream. With EARLIER already queued, these two writes:

#+begin_example
"q {"
"s\n"
#+end_example

form one =q {s= line. Stop did not execute; EARLIER survives. Reset and dispatch
can be absorbed in the same way. A following newline can establish a command
boundary while leaving the queue unknown. A later, independently requested Stop
can then clear it. Concatenating captured entries does not remove this problem.

The selected Emacs's =process-send-string= documentation explicitly permits
splitting a write into several bunches and running process output between them.
A nested heartbeat can therefore join a line too. Classifying its command as
queue-neutral is insufficient while another write is in flight.

* Reader evidence and deliberately bounded recovery

This review read all accepted Emacsvox and Omnivox ADRs together and inspected
Omnivox =31a0b9a=: =omnivox-cli/src/server.rs=, =remote.rs= and =transaction.rs=,
plus =omnivox-core/src/command.rs=. Relevant reader behavior:

| Reader | Bound and recovery |
|--------+--------------------|
| Local stdio | 524288 payload bytes, excluding LF and one trailing CR. Oversized or invalid UTF-8 records are drained through LF and discarded; later records continue. EOF can submit an unfinished final record, then closes input. |
| Remote broker | 524288 bytes including LF and any CR. Requires UTF-8 without NUL. Malformed/oversized records close the connection and retire its worker. A partial line can be retained across reads, but does not renew the lease. |
| Remote reserved prefix | Only exact =OMNIVOX-REMOTE ping= with LF or CRLF is accepted after authentication; other records starting with =OMNIVOX-REMOTE= close the connection. A ping appended to =q {= reaches the worker as queue text. |
| Legacy parser | Trims a complete record; block/space arguments are accepted. A string that looks like Stop inside another record is not a Stop command. |

The proof deliberately does not implement a second incremental remote decoder.
A complete, independently written remote packet can be checked without retaining
its text. A remote fragment across actual writes, uncertain prefix, nonlocal
write failure, unsupported encoding or overlapping write latches the proof
unusable until a fresh connection. Some such streams would happen to recover;
the client does not claim to know which ones. Fully framed opaque remote output
can instead leave only the queue unknown, allowing a later typed clear to prove
empty. Compatibility playback and the existing heartbeat lifecycle are unchanged.

* Constant-size state and transitions

Use a private low-dependency =tts-queue-state.el= service. Keep one record per
managed process, shared by both direct and aural packet writers. Fields are:
record identity, process generation, local/remote mode, queue =empty/pending/
unknown=, framing =boundary/unproven=, a sticky unusable flag, mutation serial,
and one outer-write identity plus an overlap flag. No queue text, decoder tail,
buffer, callback list or registry history belongs here. Saturating serial
exhaustion replaces the record with unknown state and a new identity; an old
guard can never match after rollover. Retirement makes the record unusable.

=boundary= means the preceding observed byte stream ends at LF, or starts at a
proven fresh input. =unproven= covers both a known unfinished line and an unknown
prefix: neither permits applying the first command's declared queue effect.
A queue may be empty while framing is unproven; that combination cannot promote.
The unusable latch is separate: recovering a trailing LF cannot undo a remote
transport fault or EOF. An unusable record stays unknown/unproven until fresh
ownership; later writes cannot publish new proof into it. Successful output describes the ordered stream if the
same worker continues consuming it, not an execution acknowledgement or playback
receipt. An asynchronous disconnect still fails delivery without replay.

For an uncontended complete return, use these transitions:

| Contribution | From boundary | From unproven |
|--------------+---------------+---------------|
| Validated single typed record ending LF | Apply its declared queue effect; remain boundary. | First record may be absorbed: queue unknown, framing boundary, serial changed. |
| Nonempty opaque entry | Queue unknown; framing boundary only if its last byte is LF. | Same. Do not infer a clear from its contents. |
| Empty entry/write | Preserve state and serial. | Preserve state and serial. |
| Partial/error/quit/throw/overlap | Queue unknown, framing unproven, serial changed. | Same. Remote also latches unusable. |
| Region output | Unknown queue and unproven framing; do not copy/read the region for proof. | Same. Remote also latches unusable. |
| EOF | Unknown queue and unproven framing; unusable for either mode. | Same. |

The successful transitions require the reviewed UTF-8 Unix output coding at the
actual write. Use =utf-8-unix= explicitly in the owned factories. Unsupported
coding invalidates proof; do not silently recode an attached connection. On a
local process a subsequent successful observed UTF-8 Unix write ending LF can
restore framing, but its first clear still cannot clear the unknown prefix.
On remote, an unusable latch stays set until a new process is owned.

Typed queue effects at a valid boundary retain the main contract: queue items
make empty pending (unknown stays unknown); unframed plain/tracked/marked dispatch,
Stop and reset clear; letter/say preserve queue but advance serial; ordinary
structured timeline, validated control and nonqueue state preserve both. Queue
operations advance serial even when the final state is empty. An absorbed
supposedly neutral record advances serial and leaves queue unknown.

For local typed records validate UTF-8 and the local payload limit. An invalid
record loses its declared effect and is opaque, even if it spells Stop. Its LF
can still restore framing because the local reader drains rejected records.
For remote, validate the entire actual packet before using any summary: start
at a boundary, end at LF, each encoded record within the inclusive limit, valid
UTF-8, no NUL, and no reserved-prefix record except exact ping. Rejecting this
proof check latches unusable; it does not reject an already selected ordinary
legacy send. New promotion must pass the check before any policy effect.

Bound proof inspection to the existing 16-MiB capture/packet budget. Oversized
unclassified output remains unclassified and invalidates proof without retaining
or fully scanning it. Local framing then remains unproven until a later bounded
write; remote latches unusable. No second full packet/encoded buffer is retained.
Use bounded per-record UTF-8 counting/scanning, discarding each temporary record.

* Compose entries before observing the actual packet

Add private effect metadata alongside delivery-entry =kind=; public delivery
arguments and generic state helpers do not confer trust. Actual constructors
validate the exact records they create. An unknown inline engine code can be
classified as a queue operation only when its outer =c= record is valid; its
engine semantics remain opaque to representation selection.

Each entry summary is a fixed six-row transfer table over queue times framing,
with a serial-disturbance bit per input. Charge added summary/fingerprint metadata
against the existing capture budget before retaining it; keep existing entry
count limits. Compose in original order; this is a
constant-size map, not a list of past commands. For example, opaque ="q {"=
followed by typed ="s\n"= maps to unknown/boundary, while a subsequent separately
typed ="s\n"= maps to empty/boundary. A multiline constructor composes its own
validated records; do not label a whole arbitrary string as one trusted Stop.

Remote wire validation applies after concatenation, independently of entry
effect composition. Adjacent fragments inside one actual complete packet can
pass framing validation; their typed effects still respect entry boundaries.
Separate actual remote writes with an unfinished tail latch unusable, even if
concatenating them later would make a valid record. Delayed scheduling retains
summaries but changes process proof only when output occurs.

A replaceable =emacsvox_tx= is one typed outer record. At a valid boundary, join
the old queue state with its validated payload's executed state; unequal states
join to unknown. Empty stays empty for a closed batch executing or being skipped;
an earlier pending queue cannot be proved emptied by a skippable frame. From an
unproven boundary the outer record itself can be absorbed, so it yields unknown.
Advance serial in either case. Existing frame admission bounds and exactly one
final plain dispatch remain mandatory.

Constructors own their command strings. Bind each entry's effect to a content
fingerprint when the effect is created; verify those fingerprints before composing
the final packet. Bind its summary to the exact process, final string identity
and packet fingerprint. Recheck content immediately before I/O and on return.
A string changed before packet construction cannot inherit an old entry effect.
Keep only fingerprints/summaries with the already retained commands, and discard
the one-use token's command reference on unwind. A pre-write mismatch invalidates
proof and fails a guarded promotion before send; a mismatch discovered after I/O
is an ambiguous failure. Neither permits a compatibility retry.

* Observe the real I/O boundary and fail closed on overlap

Install an innermost observer around =process-send-string=, plus invalidators for
=process-send-region= and =process-send-eof=. Resolve process objects, process/
buffer names, buffers and nil as the primitives do, so naming a managed process
differently cannot escape observation. Unmanaged processes pass through unchanged;
a birth-scope taint flag is the only extra observation during owned creation.
No unrelated writer is rewritten.

Private typed writes carry an exact process/string identity and one-use token.
The observer consumes it before I/O. An outer advice that rewrites arguments
cannot inherit its effect. Missing observation, changed content, unavailable
observer coverage, unsupported inner advice or a changed generation cannot
produce a success receipt. Install the observer nearest the primitive and verify
that arrangement before granting proof; do not bypass existing advice. Deliberate
replacement of Lisp/primitive machinery outside this supported arrangement is
not evidence of an observed stream. Tests wrap a fake primitive under the real
observer, including outer rewriting and missing-forwarding advice. If an outer
advice forwards unclassified bytes or returns without consuming the token, the
guarded writer cannot publish success; treat the missing receipt as ambiguous
failure, with owner retirement and no replay.

Before entering I/O mark the record in flight; no admission is permitted then.
Any same-process nested output, including a complete heartbeat/timeline, sets
an overlap flag. Neither nested completion nor outer unwind may publish an empty
proof. Keep one outer identity, not an unbounded write journal. On all nonlocal
exits, leave unknown/unproven and advance serial; remote latches unusable. Other
processes retain independent proof. A proof bookkeeping failure must never leave
old known state usable. Keep mutation/publication unwind-safe and callback-free.

A neutral write is neutral only when complete, correctly framed and outside any
other write. Neutral output from a Stop hook runs after the Stop receipt and can
preserve proof. Neutral output during that Stop is ambiguous and invalidates it.
A guarded promoted write that loses proof during I/O reports ambiguous failure,
retires owners, publishes no success/history and never replays already sent bytes.
Existing unguarded compatibility writers retain their output behavior.

* Birth, generation and reconnect

Install observation before invoking either owned process factory. A bounded
creation scope starts clean; any output to an as-yet-unregistered process during
factory execution taints it, even when made by factory advice. Conservatively
share taint with nested creation scopes; do not retain a list of unknown processes
or writes. Writes to an already managed other lane do not taint the new birth.
Immediately attach a provisional record to the returned process before waits,
callbacks, startup commands or public exposure. An old/custom attached process
starts unknown/unproven and cannot use a fresh-birth assertion.

The reviewed local =make-process= pipe path may initialize empty/boundary only
with an untainted creation scope and the reviewed coding. No user callback
occurs between testing that scope and attaching the provisional record. A tainted
local birth starts unknown/unproven; ordinary later framing/clear rules apply.

Remote attaches its provisional record immediately after network creation,
before waiting for connect or writing credentials. It starts not promotion-ready.
A private auth token is bound to that birth and the exact bounded handshake write.
Only one successful authentication write followed by the matching ready response,
without other output or overlap, consumes the fresh-worker grant and initializes
empty/boundary. Ready alone cannot clear an existing queue or fault. Do not retain
or log credentials in proof metadata. Unexpected pre-ready writes or birth taint
make this remote record unusable for promotion; ordinary connection code keeps
its current success/failure behavior. Start typed heartbeats after this handoff.

Assigning the public TTS generation attaches it to the same provisional identity;
it never resets observed queue/framing/serial or erases a fault. Unexpected
reassignment to an existing process invalidates proof. New local/remote processes
get different identities, and old receipts, timers and closures cannot alter them.
Registry/capability replies and playback completion never establish emptiness.

* Admission and exact own-stop receipt

A proposal replacing named queues stores one private guard in its existing
preparation: proof-record identity, generation, serial and the acknowledged
registration identity. It requires empty, boundary, usable and no write in flight
at selection, before policy interruption and immediately before output. Registry
validation and existing owner lifecycle checks remain independent requirements.
Ordinary already-structured speech keeps its existing admission path. Bounded
compatibility diagnostics distinguish input-boundary-unproven and remote-proof-
unusable from an ordinary cross-call pending queue. Explanations remain separate
from actual-playback receipts and do not repeat warnings during routine speech.

Pending/unknown/unproven/unusable at selection chooses compatibility. A proposed
future Stop cannot justify promotion. Once a layered proposal and owners are
prepared, a failed recheck aborts; do not rebuild legacy output after policy effects.

Only the exact ready preparation's one permitted policy Stop can advance its
expected serial. Bind a private permission to that preparation and exact typed
write token. The completed receipt must prove that Stop started at boundary,
finished without overlap and left empty/boundary on the same identity/generation.
Publish the expected serial once, before owner cleanup and stopped hooks. A failed
or merely boundary-restoring Stop cannot advance it. Recheck after hooks and
before writing the proposal. Public Stop still cancels, and nested dispatch,
queue, opaque or letter output invalidates the saved serial even if it restores
empty. Post-Stop complete neutral output can preserve it; in-flight neutral output
cannot. This narrows the original contract's ambiguous phrase "nested neutral".
