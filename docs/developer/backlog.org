** TODO Consider targeted cancellation of EAT speech already sent to Omnivox

Deferred by the maintainer on 2026-10-05. The chosen foreground autospeak
contract is Emacsvox-only: losing focus or switching autospeak off suppresses
new automatic feedback and discards locally pending work. Speech already sent
to Omnivox may finish. This contract governs foreground feedback in
[[file:../../lisp/integrations/emacsvox-eat-core.el][the EAT integration]].

A possible future extension would cancel queued or active speech belonging to
that terminal without interrupting manual reading, another terminal, or the
notification lane. This needs a negotiated Omnivox operation scoped to the
delivery domain, with a generation boundary to protect newer work after focus
returns. Specify compatibility and test delayed cancellation before adopting
it. [[file:../../lisp/aural/emacsvox-aural-transport.el][Local pending-delivery cancellation]] alone cannot provide that guarantee;
a process-wide stop is not a substitute for targeted cancellation.

** TODO Refine punctuation speech behavior

Deliver the work in the two stages described by the
[[https://github.com/bartbunting/omnivox/blob/v1.15.0/docs/plans/punctuation-configuration.md][Omnivox punctuation plan]]. Stage one configures the existing =none=, =some= and
=all= tables on the speech host, including common Unicode punctuation and sparse
pronunciation overrides. Emacsvox retains its mode/buffer level selection;
Org's default =some= preserves apostrophes unless the host table is overridden.
Omnivox 1.15.0 names straight, curly and modifier apostrophes at =all= by default,
without requiring a configuration file.

The follow-up =omnivox-punctuation= interface reviews host-provided defaults and
saved overrides, edits independent drafts, saves through the negotiated local
service, and offers a separate explicit speech restart. The settings menu exposes it
under /Voices and speech/. File conflicts retain the draft. Remote and
native-direct editing remain outside the existing local management provider.

Stage two is implemented in development: named profile drafts on the host,
catalogues from both workers, mode/buffer selections and retained built-in
fallbacks for older, absent or disagreeing servers. Listening acceptance remains.
The proposed =most= level is also deferred beyond Omnivox 1.15.0; its exact
defaults and compatibility behavior need coordinated host/client work.
Keep character inspection independent of prose levels. Collect representative
prose, code, shell and structured-text listening examples before further default
changes; automated preparation checks do not establish audible acceptance.

[[file:../engine-management-design.org][engine-management design]] and Omnivox's

Listening acceptance of saved changes and failed-Apply recovery remains.

engines on or off, and choose preferred and backup engines. Include permission
for automatic selection, a check and spoken sample, and an advanced section for
startup arguments and waiting times. Reuse the Voice Workbench for choosing
voices and adjusting how they sound.

restart speech; provide an explicit Apply action that checks changes and uses
the same prepared settings for normal and notification speech. Verify safe saving,
failed-Apply recovery and clear spoken feedback during listening acceptance. Preserve
unrelated settings and explain when another setting takes priority.

Settings belong to the computer running Omnivox, including Windows when Emacs
runs under WSL. Start with local configuration; remote editing needs separate
design and authorization. Engine downloads and new language-selection rules
remain outside this item.
