#+title: Feedback inspection consistency review
#+date: 2026-11-13

* Scope and result

Reviewed retained and current-item input across Explain, Recent Feedback,
direct voice/earcon remapping and Change Feedback. The investigation found
one source-association defect in guided multipart editing. Existing checks
cover the other boundaries below; this review adds no listening acceptance.

* Corrected source association

When a retained presentation's first part had no source identity, Change
Feedback copied that part's absent source into every later selection. A later
part could therefore lose buffer scope even when its original buffer survived.
Direct remapping selected and associated that later part correctly.

Both paths now use =emacsvox-aural-tools--retained-plan-input= to assemble the
selected part's frozen facts, context and rendering, and associate its source
independently. Association requires the original buffer identity. Renaming or
editing the surviving buffer does not invalidate historical inspection; a
killed buffer, same-name replacement or missing identity cannot acquire scope.
Switching back to a source-less part releases buffer scope again.

The regression =emacsvox-aural-guided-history-selects-source-per-part= in
[[file:../../test/emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-tests.el][the guided-feedback tests]] failed before the fix and passes for original,
renamed, edited, killed and replacement buffers. It compares direct and guided
selection and exercises actual buffer-scoped application.

* Preserved boundaries

| Boundary | Evidence and behaviour |
|----------+------------------------|
| Multipart inspection | [[file:../../test/emacsvox-aural-transport-tests.el][Transport tests]] retain a real native transaction and inspect/remap later parts. Explain retains additional plans; [[file:../../test/emacsvox-aural-feedback-details-tests.el][Feedback Details tests]] cover distinct recorded voices and linked editing. |
| Selection and replay | Guided tests preserve playback order beyond nine parts, frozen voices, exact selected-part replay and independent drafts. |
| Truncated and older records | Explanation labels incomplete retention as a preview. Guided editing rejects truncated baselines; Feedback Details guards replay and supports older records. |
| Current source item | [[file:../../test/emacsvox-aural-tools-tests.el][Tools tests]] reject earlier-row, replaced-item and edited-item matches. Markers follow insertion before the item; prepared edits recheck their source before saving. |
| Historical source | Direct and guided remapping require frozen buffer identity, without imposing current-item freshness on historical facts. Buffer names alone never establish identity. |
| Storage ownership | History remains frozen bounded data. The new helper places live source references only in temporary editor input, leaving retained plans unchanged. |

* Shared inspection follow-up

The subsequent [[file:feedback-inspection-model.org][shared inspection implementation]] consolidates retained-part
selection, voice descriptions, actions, status and historical source association.
It preserves the current-item and historical validity policies established
here. Overlay-only invalidation remains a separate design question requiring
a concrete reproduction and contract.
