* Result

Explain, Recent Feedback, direct remapping, Change Feedback and Feedback
Details now share a private inspection view and helpers in
[[file:../../lisp/aural/emacsvox-aural-inspection.el][the inspection service]].  The extraction preserves prompts, summary wording,
part order, source validity and edit eligibility.  It follows the earlier
[[file:inspection-consistency-review.org][consistency review]]; it does not claim another confirmed user-facing defect.

Local master was first fast-forwarded to =684a76a13=, integrating the completed
delivery, recording and voice Apply work.  This cleanup is developed separately
on =refactor/feedback-inspection=.

* Shared data and interface boundaries

The view carries the complete frozen plan list, explanation basis, selected
one-based part number and editing component.  It shares existing plan data
without copying payloads or changing the retained record.  Summaries are
computed on demand.  No view, rendered summary, buffer or marker is added to
history, and ordinary speech delivery constructs no inspection views.

Common helpers own action order, cue choices with their original phase and
position, voice descriptions, degradation and semantic fallback counts, and
semantic field grouping.  Feedback interfaces no longer construct temporary
history records merely to describe a selected voice.  Explain adapts its
existing first-plan and additional-plan fields to the view; those accessors
and the existing private remapping wrappers remain available.

Interface behaviour remains explicit.  Direct remapping selects a sole
eligible part automatically and prompts when several qualify.  The guided
editor still requires a part choice for multipart feedback and preserves each
part's draft.  Silent content remains eligible for direct voice remapping;
Feedback Details retains its spoken-content guard for the compact voice
action.  Short rows, completion labels and full explanations keep their own
formatting and verbosity.

Historical input is associated with the selected part's original buffer
identity.  Renaming or editing a surviving buffer does not invalidate frozen
history; a killed or same-name replacement buffer cannot acquire its scope.
Current-item lookup retains the stricter identity, point and modification-tick
checks, and prepared edits still recheck their captured source guards.  Live
buffers and markers remain in temporary interface or editor state.

Playback, history bounds, recorder limits and source extraction retain their
existing owners.  Overlay-only invalidation and the separate presentation
contract decisions in the backlog remain outside this behaviour-preserving
extraction.

and explicit part selection; exact action positions; selected editor input; and
an unchanged serialised history record after inspection.

and transport.  Source checks pass on Emacs 31.0.90 and 30.2: 370 passed, with
