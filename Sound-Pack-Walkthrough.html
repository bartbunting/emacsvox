6.8.1 Walkthrough: Try A Sound Pack ¶

  1. Open C-e H, choose Sound packs, and note the currently active pack. Select another pack of kind sound and press P for a representative cue. Preview does not select the pack.
  2. Press RET to browse its cues. Inspect Availability and Provider: a cue may come from the pack itself, its parent, or a registered fallback. Press P on a few cues you recognize. S stops playback.
  3. Press s to return to the pack manager, then v to validate the selected pack. Missing files and unresolved cues need attention before relying on the pack. Press a to activate it and revisit a familiar action, such as opening a list or moving between sections.
  4. To retain this selection, open Home’s Presentation profiles and save the current setup with N, or deliberately update the selected profile with w. Review its other fields: this saves the selected setup, not just the sound pack. See Save, Switch, and Share Your Setup.
  5. To restore the original, return to Sound packs and activate the pack you noted at the start. If you saved the experiment in a profile, restore or update that profile as well. Preview alone requires no restoration.

If only one cue is unwanted, change the rule using that cue rather than changing the entire pack. The next walkthrough demonstrates this.
