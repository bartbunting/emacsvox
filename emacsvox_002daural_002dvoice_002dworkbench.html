15.53 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench ¶

A single accessible workbench presents logical voices, physical inventory, engines, and portable style/effect information. Transactional previews use the same sample text without changing saved palettes or routing profiles.

15.53.1.1 emacsvox-aural-prefer-engine ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-prefer-engine (engine-id &optional save) ¶
C-e d e ¶
Prefer ENGINE-ID globally while preserving explicit voice routes.

By default the change lasts for this Emacs session.  With prefix argument
SAVE, atomically save it in the active routing profile.  Remaining engines
keep their relative order; explicit logical-voice routes and fallback policy
are unchanged.  The command does not stop speech already playing.

(fn ENGINE-ID &optional SAVE)
