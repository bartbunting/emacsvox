3.7 Voice Lock Mode ¶

Voice Lock is enabled automatically in supported buffers. It maps faces and other structural text properties to speech personalities, allowing comments, strings, headings, links, and other elements to sound distinct without adding words to the document.

The exact personalities depend on the selected speech engine and your Aural Presentation settings. Try Voice Lock in source code, Org, Info, or EWW to hear the active mode’s structure.

To disable Voice Lock globally, run M-x global-voice-lock-mode or add this form to your Emacs init file: 

(global-voice-lock-mode -1)

The current binding and command reference are in Emacsvox Keymaps in Emacsvox Reference Manual. For detailed configuration, see Voice Lock. For historical context on Aural CSS, see Audio Formatting And Aural CSS in Emacspeak Heritage.
