15.20 emacsvox-aural-doctor ¶

Diagnose the running aural installation and configuration without starting a speech server or changing presentation state. Safe, deterministic repairs are explicit commands from the spoken report.

15.20.1.1 emacsvox-aural-doctor ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-doctor ¶
Open the spoken Aural Doctor and announce its overall result.

15.20.1.2 emacsvox-aural-doctor-help ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-doctor-help ¶
Display and speak Aural Doctor help.

15.20.1.3 emacsvox-aural-doctor-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-doctor-mode ¶
Spoken diagnostics for aural presentation.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-doctor-mode-hook’, as the final or penultimate
step during initialization.

Key             Binding
——————————————————————————-
?		emacsvox-aural-doctor-help
h		emacsvox-aural
r		emacsvox-aural-doctor-repair-current
