#+title: Accent sound pack

Accent, customised by Bart Bunting, builds on the =3d= pack inherited from
Emacspeak, whose principal original author is T. V. Raman.

This directory contains Bart's 17 replacement recordings. The other 38 cues
come from the bundled =3d= pack through inheritance. The replacements are
mono; inherited recordings retain the 3d pack's spatial metadata.

Use =M-x emacsvox-sounds-select-theme RET accent RET= to select this pack.
You can preview its cues in =M-x emacsvox-aural-list-sound-packs=.
A personal pack at =~/.emacsvox/sounds/packs/accent/= takes precedence over
this bundled copy.
