#+title: Accent sound pack

Accent, customised by Bart Bunting, builds on the =3d= pack inherited from
Emacspeak, whose principal original author is T. V. Raman.

This directory contains 18 replacement recordings. The other 37 cues
come from the bundled =3d= pack through inheritance. The replacements are
mono; inherited recordings retain the 3d pack's spatial metadata.

The =repeat-start= cue uses =mechanical-clack-01.ogg= for menu wrapping.

The =recordings/= directory preserves four original sound files:

- =mechanical-clack-01.ogg= supplies =repeat-start=.
- =soft-impact-01.ogg= supplies =section=.
- =double-knock-01.ogg= and =soft-impact-02.ogg= are available for future cue
  assignments.

Only the files directly in this pack directory are assigned to cues; the
original recordings are not separate entries in the cue picker.

Use =M-x emacsvox-sounds-select-theme RET accent RET= to select this pack.
You can preview its cues in =M-x emacsvox-aural-list-sound-packs=.
A personal pack at =~/.emacsvox/sounds/packs/accent/= takes precedence over
this bundled copy.
