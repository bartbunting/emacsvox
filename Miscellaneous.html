5.8.3 Stop Or Restart Speech ¶

Stopping ends current output without disabling future feedback. Quiet mode lets you work temporarily without speech. Restart after correcting a backend problem or changing the selected synthesiser, not as an assumed cure for every missing integration.

C-e s

Stop current main and notification speech with tts-stop.

C-e d q

Toggle current-buffer quiet mode; C-u toggles it globally.

C-e C-s

Stop and restart the selected speech server.

C-e d V

Speak adapter and version information.

Server selection, repeated-pattern cleanup, text splitting, language selection, and direct engine controls have specialist Help and reference entries. For missing speech, follow Recovering From No Speech.
