8.2.1 Listen without changing settings ¶

This exercise previews an installed voice without changing saved settings. Start in an ordinary working buffer so the menu has a source to return to.

  1. Press C-e H v b to open the engine browser through Voices and engines.
  2. Select an available engine and press v to list its voices. RET on an Omnivox engine opens its status, downloads, and settings instead.
  3. Move to an available voice and press P to hear a sample. S stops it.
  4. Use q to return through the browser’s parent views to Voices and engines. Once back in the menu, C-q closes the menu sequence. The original buffer remains your working context.

Browsing and previewing save nothing. A downloaded but inactive voice may need enablement and Apply before it can be sampled; choose an already available voice for this first exercise. If an engine is unavailable, its details explain why. If speech itself is absent, begin with Recovering From No Speech.
