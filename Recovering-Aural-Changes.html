6.11.1 Recover Unfinished Changes ¶

Use h to leave a working editor through Home without losing it. Home’s Resume unfinished changes opens the original draft, including its source and working settings. In the common voice editor, u undoes and q offers to keep the draft, discard changes, or continue. Guided feedback q goes back one level, then hides and retains the draft at the top level. To undo an already applied rule, open Your feedback rules, select it in the appropriate scope, and use t to disable it or d to remove it.

Retained drafts last only until Emacs exits. Discarding a draft abandons unsaved edits; it does not undo a completed save. A voice editor reports saved settings separately from successful application to the speech server. Keep that distinction when deciding whether to retry or to edit the saved voice.

Applying waits for each speech stream to acknowledge its configuration. If an Omnivox apply times out, the Workbench reports server outcome unconfirmed. Emacsvox stops that apply and sends no later steps, but changes already sent may still take effect on the server. A timeout does not undo the saved engine settings or confirm that the server rejected them. Press r to reapply the saved engine settings and obtain a fresh result. Save or cancel any newer staged edits before retrying. A partial result retains the streams that acknowledged success and identifies those that timed out.
