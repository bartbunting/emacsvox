6.10.2 Choose What Persists ¶

These actions answer different questions:

  • Preview plays an example without saving or selecting the draft.
  • Retain draft keeps unfinished edits in this Emacs session only.
  • Save to collection without selecting saves the voice for later use.
  • Save and apply saves the voice and selects its palette for this session.
  • Activate selects an existing palette or sound pack now.
  • Use at Startup in Voice Palettes changes the palette selected by the saved startup profile; it does not apply that profile now.
  • Presentation Profiles save selected options, palette, sound pack, and spatial settings together. Their referenced resources remain shared.
  • Export and Full backup create files to share or retain separately.

A saved personal rule is used in future sessions. Session and buffer rules expire with their session or buffer. Saving a profile does not turn these temporary rules or unfinished drafts into permanent settings.
