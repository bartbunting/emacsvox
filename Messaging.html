9.6 Messaging ¶

For indexed mail search, asynchronous results, threads, attachments, and privacy controls, follow Notmuch Mail. For another mail or messaging client, start with its own manual and mode help, then find the corresponding Emacsvox module in the generated reference. The presence of a module is not a substitute for configuring the underlying account or service.
