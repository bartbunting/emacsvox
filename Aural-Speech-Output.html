6.9 Speech Output And Notifications ¶

Speech rate and output settings control how speech reaches you, independently of which presentation rule chose the content. Speech rate in Home applies to the captured source buffer; invoke that action with a prefix argument to set the global rate. A named voice’s relative rate adjusts its speech around the applicable base rate. See Voice Adjustments.

Emacsvox assigns speech to one of two streams according to what caused it:

  • The main stream presents foreground information produced directly by your current interaction, such as movement, entering a buffer, or opening a message. New navigation interrupts obsolete main-stream speech, and you can interrupt a longer entry announcement by navigating again.
  • The notification stream presents asynchronous events, such as completion of background work. It does not stop main-stream speech, and normal navigation does not stop it, so both streams may be heard concurrently. A notification cue and its associated speech are delivered together on this stream.

Open Notification output in Home to choose the arrangement in Customize. With Omnivox, Automatic normally uses an independent notification process. The left, right, and both choices select its output route. Use main output (notifications remain audible) sends notifications through the main process instead. Navigation can then interrupt them; this setting does not mute notifications. Other backends may use their configured device or fall back to the main stream.

After changing the setting, initialize the arrangement with C-e d C-n. Use Customize’s save action to retain the choice across restarts. In Lisp, default or an empty string selects main output; prefer the descriptive Customize labels for ordinary configuration.

Stop notification speech in Home stops the current notification stream; it is not a persistent mute. Review notifications opens the notification history so you can read an announcement you missed.

Stopping obsolete main speech is local to the main stream. An explicit tts-stop command stops both main and notification speech.
