6.7.2 Walkthrough: Keep An Installed Voice Experiment ¶

This example keeps the physical voice and its tuning together. First create reading-demo as in Make a Personal Reading Palette, or choose an existing personal destination. Note the active palette before beginning.

  1. In the installed-voice browser, open an experiment with t, adjust a field, and use P or B to listen. Press w when ready to keep it.
  2. At Destination palette, choose your personal palette. At Use for named voice, choose bolden. Select Physical voice and adjustments — this choice, then Add as preferred. These choices put the experiment first while retaining existing alternative physical choices.
  3. The named-voice editor opens with the proposed combination. Nothing has been saved yet. Expand Fallbacks: the new first choice should be marked customized, with any previous explicit choices after it. P plays the whole proposed voice. Review the reported engine and physical voice after the sample; an unavailable preferred choice can cause a fallback.
  4. Press w to save and apply. Wait for the editor’s result. If it reports a partial save or failed application, keep the draft and follow Recovering Unfinished Changes. A successful save selects the destination palette for this session. Use U in Voice Palettes only if you also want that palette at startup.
  5. To reverse this change before saving, use u or discard the draft. After saving, reopen the named voice, expand Fallbacks, open the added choice’s action menu with RET, choose Remove, preview, and save. Removing that row retains the other choices and shared settings. Reactivate your original palette if you also want to restore the session selection.

To tune a fallback independently, open its action menu and choose Customize this voice. Set one field, audition that choice, then select Play whole fallback chain to test normal selection. Its custom field replaces the shared value only when that choice is used. i returns the field to the shared value. Reordering a choice carries its adjustments with it.
