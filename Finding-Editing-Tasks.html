16.2 Edit and rearrange text ¶

  • Hear exactly what I selected: establish point and mark and inspect the newline boundary before an operation.
  • Copy text into another document: keep the source intact and check the insertion and kill-ring contents at the destination.
  • Move a paragraph: cut a checked passage and verify both its old and new positions, including blank lines.
  • Paste something I copied earlier: distinguish the kill-ring chooser from replacing the last yank with an earlier entry.
  • Undo and listen between corrections: use the right buffer’s undo history without accidentally redoing earlier undos.
  • Copy between Emacs and another application: test the clipboard boundary without assuming that Windows, WSL and terminal sessions work identically.
  • Replace only the occurrences I choose: limit the operation to a checked region and review each proposed change before saving.
