8.8 Desktop Management ¶

Emacs buffers, windows, frames, and tabs provide the workspace. Their standard commands receive speech feedback, and the active help describes any additional mode-specific bindings. Use C-x b to select a buffer and C-h m in specialized buffer-list or tab interfaces to learn their local commands.
