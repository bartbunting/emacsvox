8.13.5 Review changed settings before editing again ¶

Press C-e H d to inspect changes without making another one. The snapshot separates captured-buffer values, global defaults and saved startup values known to Customize. It includes loaded Emacsvox, TTS and Omnivox options that are saved, buffer-local or nonstandard. Saved expressions appear as recorded. Press RET to read a complete row with its scope, g to refresh the snapshot, and q to return.

Editor and cue drafts appear separately. The snapshot does not reread files, query the speech host, establish that saved data is active or include options from unloaded integrations. Voice, rule and host-pronunciation stores retain their own saving rules. Return to the owning editor or menu to change, save, apply or restart anything.
