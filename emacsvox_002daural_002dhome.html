15.25.1.5 emacsvox-aural-home-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-home-mode ¶
Spoken home mode for aural presentation discovery and interaction.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-home-mode-hook’, as the final or penultimate step
during initialization.
