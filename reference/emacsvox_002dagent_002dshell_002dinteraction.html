2.19 emacsvox-agent-shell-interaction ¶

Handle questions, session settings, steering, push, queue and compose outcomes. Keep callback generations and interaction snapshots with cleanup. The facade owns advice installation and activation; event subscriptions call these helpers. Load through ‘emacsvox-agent-shell’. Loading definitions installs no advice or buffer hooks.
