2.17 emacsvox-aural-compiler ¶

Compile resolved render plans to frozen resources, voices, spatial values, and backend-ready ordered actions. This module performs no queueing or speech-server process control.

  • emacsvox-aural-compiler Options

2.17.1 emacsvox-aural-compiler Options ¶

Variable: User Option emacsvox-aural-unsupported-volume-policy ¶
Policy for explicit volume when the active transport cannot apply it.

‘degrade’ queues the presentation without volume and records the exact
capability degradation.  ‘reject’ signals an error before anything is queued.

Default Value: 

degrade
