2.304 emacsvox-text-tree ¶

Shared, conservative interpretation of complete text trees. Callers own the decision that a region is eligible: this library installs no hooks, reads no renderer properties and starts no speech. Node offsets and depth are retained independently of speech cleanup for later navigation adapters.
