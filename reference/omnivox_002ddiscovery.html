2.348 omnivox-discovery ¶

Discover physical voices locally or from the connected workstation and request inventory refreshes through the voice adapter’s control service. The adapter retains negotiation, request coalescing and response handling, including the routing registration that follows an inventory response. Loading these definitions does not run discovery or start speech processes.
