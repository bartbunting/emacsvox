2.43.1.4 emacsvox-aural-semantics-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-semantics-mode ¶
Major mode for browsing registered aural semantics.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-semantics-mode-hook’, as the final or penultimate
step during initialization.

Key             Binding
——————————————————————————-
RET		emacsvox-describe-aural-semantic
?		emacsvox-aural-semantics-help
h		emacsvox-aural
