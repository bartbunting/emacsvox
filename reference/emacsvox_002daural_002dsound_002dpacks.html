2.44 emacsvox-aural-sound-packs ¶

Accessible sound-pack and cue browsers. Users can inspect native, inherited, fallback, and missing assets; audition concrete cues; activate packs; validate coverage; open directories; and edit safe discovered-pack manifests without editing raw Lisp.
