2.54 emacsvox-aural-voice-context ¶

2.54.1.1 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-adjust ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-adjust ¶
Prepare a dimension-only rule with an explicit match and lifetime.

2.54.1.2 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-adjust-rule ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-adjust-rule ¶
Adjust one voice field in the selected rule; Inherit removes that field.

2.54.1.3 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-choose ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-choose ¶
Choose a new current source item from the originating base voice editor.

2.54.1.4 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-compare ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-compare ¶
Compare original and edited voices against the same captured context.

2.54.1.5 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-mode ¶
Inspect contextual voice requests independently of the named base draft.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-interface-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-voice-context-mode-hook’, as the final or
penultimate step during initialization.

2.54.1.6 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-next ¶

2.54.1.7 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-open ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-open (&optional choose-source) ¶
Inspect the base draft against a captured source item, without saving.
With CHOOSE-SOURCE, explicitly select a buffer and use its current point.

(fn &optional CHOOSE-SOURCE)

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-play ¶

2.54.1.9 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-previous ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-previous ¶

2.54.1.10 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-recapture ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-refresh ¶

2.54.1.12 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-return ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-return ¶

2.54.1.13 emacsvox-aural-voice-context-stop ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-context-stop ¶
