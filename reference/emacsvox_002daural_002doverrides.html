2.27 emacsvox-aural-overrides ¶

Present personal, session, and source-buffer rules as one accessible view. This manager does not introduce another presentation layer: it explains and mutates the existing strongest three layers in the aural cascade.

2.27.1.8 emacsvox-aural-overrides-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-overrides-mode ¶
Spoken manager for personal, session, and buffer presentation overrides.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-overrides-mode-hook’, as the final or penultimate
step during initialization.
