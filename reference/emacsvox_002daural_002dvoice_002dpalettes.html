2.59.1.7 emacsvox-aural-voice-palette-previews-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-palette-previews-mode ¶
Spoken browser for effective voices in one palette.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-voice-palette-previews-mode-hook’, as the final
or penultimate step during initialization.
