2.278 emacsvox-speak-completion ¶

Completion reading and navigation. The core speech facade retains activation in its established order.

  • Emacsvox-Speak-Completion Commands

2.278.1 Emacsvox-Speak-Completion Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-completions-move-to-completion-group
  • emacsvox-speak-completions-if-available
  • emacsvox-switch-to-reference-buffer

2.278.1.1 emacsvox-completions-move-to-completion-group ¶

Command: emacsvox-completions-move-to-completion-group ¶
Move to group of choices beginning with character last
typed. If no such group exists, then we try to search for that
char, or dont move.

2.278.1.2 emacsvox-speak-completions-if-available ¶

Command: emacsvox-speak-completions-if-available ¶
C-e M-SPC ¶
Speak completions if available.

2.278.1.3 emacsvox-switch-to-reference-buffer ¶

Command: emacsvox-switch-to-reference-buffer ¶
Switch back to buffer that generated completions.
