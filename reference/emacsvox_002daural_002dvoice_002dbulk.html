2.55 emacsvox-aural-voice-bulk ¶

One unsaved review owns a complete palette proposal. Private previews and coordinated persistence reuse the common voice editor’s existing services.

2.55.1.8 emacsvox-aural-voice-bulk-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-bulk-mode ¶
Review a physical voice across every named voice before saving.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-voice-bulk-mode-hook’, as the final or
penultimate step during initialization.
