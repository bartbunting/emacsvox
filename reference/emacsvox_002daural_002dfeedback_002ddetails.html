2.26 emacsvox-aural-feedback-details ¶

A stable, readable report of one retained presentation. Semantic fields group their formatting runs, and linked editors keep independent drafts. Original replay uses frozen output; proposed playback uses the normal planner with private rule layers and captured source context.

2.26.1.3 emacsvox-aural-feedback-details-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-feedback-details-mode ¶
Read and change a pinned presentation at your own pace.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-interface-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-feedback-details-mode-hook’, as the final or
penultimate step during initialization.
