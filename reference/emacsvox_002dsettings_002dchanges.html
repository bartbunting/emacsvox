2.228 emacsvox-settings-changes ¶

A read-only snapshot of loaded Custom options and their separate draft stores.

  • Emacsvox-Settings-Changes Commands

2.228.1 Emacsvox-Settings-Changes Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-settings-changes
  • emacsvox-settings-changes-mode
  • emacsvox-settings-changes-refresh

2.228.1.1 emacsvox-settings-changes ¶

Command: emacsvox-settings-changes ¶
Review current values, saved defaults and drafts without changing them.

2.228.1.2 emacsvox-settings-changes-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-settings-changes-mode ¶
Read changed loaded options and retained drafts; g refreshes, q returns.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-settings-changes-mode-hook’, as the final or
penultimate step during initialization.

Key             Binding
——————————————————————————-
RET		emacsvox-settings-changes–speak

2.228.1.3 emacsvox-settings-changes-refresh ¶

Command: emacsvox-settings-changes-refresh ¶
Refresh the snapshot while keeping the selected setting and column.
