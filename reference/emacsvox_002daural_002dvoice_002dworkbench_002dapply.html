2.83 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-apply ¶

Voice workbench save and apply. Load through ‘emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench’.

  • Emacsvox-Aural-Voice-Workbench-Apply Commands

2.83.1 Emacsvox-Aural-Voice-Workbench-Apply Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-retry-apply
  • emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-save-and-apply
  • emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-undo-applied

2.83.1.1 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-retry-apply ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-retry-apply ¶
Idempotently retry the committed profile on all live speech streams.

2.83.1.2 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-save-and-apply ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-save-and-apply ¶
Atomically save the staged profile and apply it to every speech stream.

2.83.1.3 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-undo-applied ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-undo-applied ¶
Restore and apply the previous known-good profile revision.
