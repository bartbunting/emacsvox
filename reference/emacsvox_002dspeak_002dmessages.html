2.280 emacsvox-speak-messages ¶

Message history and replay. The core speech facade retains activation in its established order.

  • Emacsvox-Speak-Messages Commands

2.280.1 Emacsvox-Speak-Messages Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-speak-message-again
  • emacsvox-toggle-speak-messages
  • emacsvox-view-notifications

2.280.1.1 emacsvox-speak-message-again ¶

Command: emacsvox-speak-message-again (&optional from-message-cache) ¶
C-e a ¶
C-e <backspace> ¶
C-e <delete> ¶
C-e M-a ¶
Repeat the pending question, or the last message from Emacs.
During a multiple-choice question, include its full explanation and
choices.  Otherwise read the last Messages line, or the cached message
with prefix FROM-MESSAGE-CACHE.  Interactive use also copies the text
to the kill ring.

(fn &optional FROM-MESSAGE-CACHE)

2.280.1.2 emacsvox-toggle-speak-messages ¶

Command: emacsvox-toggle-speak-messages (&optional prefix) ¶
C-e C-M-q ¶
Toggle  state of whether emacsvox echoes messages.

2.280.1.3 emacsvox-view-notifications ¶

Command: emacsvox-view-notifications ¶
C-h N ¶
<f1> N ¶
<help> N ¶
Display notifications.
