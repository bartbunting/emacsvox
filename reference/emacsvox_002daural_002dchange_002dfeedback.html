2.17 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback ¶

Guide a narrowly scoped change using existing semantic facts and rule data. Preview, match, and lifetime are independent choices. No live rule layer changes until Apply; the advanced editor remains available for full control.

2.17.1.3 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-apply ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-apply ¶

2.17.1.4 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-change ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-change ¶

2.17.1.6 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-help ¶

2.17.1.7 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-lifetime ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-lifetime ¶

2.17.1.8 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-match ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-match ¶

2.17.1.9 emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-mode ¶
Guide an unapplied change to selected feedback.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-change-feedback-mode-hook’, as the final or
penultimate step during initialization.
