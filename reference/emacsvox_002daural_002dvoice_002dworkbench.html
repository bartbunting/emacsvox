2.82 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench ¶

A single accessible workbench presents logical voices, physical inventory, engines, and portable style/effect information. Transactional previews use the same sample text without changing saved palettes or routing profiles. This entry point owns buffer setup, navigation, refresh and hook registration. Separate modules own views, preview, library integration, routing and apply.

2.82.1.8 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-mode ¶
Spoken workbench for logical styles and physical voice routing.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode
‘emacsvox-aural-tabulated-mode’ might have run, this mode runs the
hook ‘emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-mode-hook’, as the final or
penultimate step during initialization.

Downloaded means catalogue packages; Enabled means desired library selection.

(fn &optional FILTER)

2.82.1.12 emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-quit ¶

Command: emacsvox-aural-voice-workbench-quit ¶
Return from an engine’s voice list, or dismiss the workbench.

When an engine row opened this voice list, restore its selected row and
column.  A dedicated browser returns to its engine details.  Otherwise
hide the workbench, retaining staged edits and warning
when they remain unsaved.
