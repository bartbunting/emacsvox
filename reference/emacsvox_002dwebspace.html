2.241.1.1 emacsvox-webspace-feed-reader ¶

Command: emacsvox-webspace-feed-reader (&optional refresh) ¶
C-. R ¶
C-x @ s R ¶
Display Feed Reader Feed list in a WebSpace buffer.
Optional interactive prefix arg forces a refresh.

(fn &optional REFRESH)

2.241.1.6 emacsvox-webspace-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-webspace-mode ¶
Major mode for Webspace interaction.

Key             Binding
——————————————————————————-
TAB		forward-button
’		emacsvox-speak-rest-of-buffer
.		emacsvox-webspace-filter
/		search-forward
<		beginning-of-buffer
>		end-of-buffer
?		search-backward
b		backward-button
f		forward-button
n		forward-button
p		backward-button
q		bury-buffer
y		emacsvox-webspace-yank-link
<backtab>	backward-button

C-M-i		backward-button

In addition to any hooks its parent mode ‘special-mode’ might have
run, this mode runs the hook ‘emacsvox-webspace-mode-hook’, as the
final or penultimate step during initialization.
