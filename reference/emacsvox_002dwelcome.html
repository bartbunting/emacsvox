2.255 emacsvox-welcome ¶

A short, keyboard-accessible introduction. The startup preference belongs to the Emacs profile, independently of the application installation.

2.255.1.1 emacsvox-welcome ¶

Command: emacsvox-welcome ¶
C-h W ¶
<f1> W ¶
<help> W ¶
<menu-bar> <help-menu> <emacsvox-welcome> ¶
Open the Emacsvox getting-started screen and speak a short introduction.

2.255.1.2 emacsvox-welcome-mode ¶

Command: emacsvox-welcome-mode ¶
Getting started.  TAB and Shift-TAB choose actions; RET activates; q returns.

In addition to any hooks its parent mode ‘special-mode’ might have
run, this mode runs the hook ‘emacsvox-welcome-mode-hook’, as the
final or penultimate step during initialization.

Key             Binding
——————————————————————————-
TAB		emacsvox-welcome-next
RET		push-button
SPC		push-button

0 .. 9		digit-argument

>		end-of-buffer

g		revert-buffer

2.255.2 emacsvox-welcome Options ¶

Variable: User Option emacsvox-welcome-show-at-startup ¶
Whether to offer the welcome screen at startup.
The screen’s toggle remembers an explicit choice in this Emacs profile.
A saved choice takes precedence over this default.  Explicitly opened files,
automated speech checks, and sessions already in use keep their current buffer.

Default Value: 

t
