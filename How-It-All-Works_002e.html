6.2 How Voice Lock Fits The Presentation ¶

The normal path has four parts:

  1. The active Emacs mode marks meaningful text with faces. An Emacsvox integration may also attach a named speech personality directly.
  2. While Voice Lock is active, the established face-and-personality mapping supplies a compatibility voice for the spoken text.
  3. Aural Presentation can combine that compatibility voice with semantic rules. Separate visual-face rules can also participate whether Voice Lock is on or off. A voice palette gives the resulting logical voices portable characteristics and routes them to available physical voices.
  4. The selected speech adapter translates the result for its synthesizer.

The meaning of a distinction can therefore remain stable while its exact sound varies with the engine and voice inventory. A high-quality engine may express several characteristics; a simpler engine can fall back to a smaller set without changing the document or its mode.

Older help and configuration may show names such as voice-bolden, voice-monotone, or generated acss-... symbols. They are compatibility details below the user-facing presentation system. Historical background on their Aural CSS design is preserved in Audio Formatting And Aural CSS in Emacspeak Heritage.
