15.186 emacsvox-proced ¶

PROCED == Process Editor A new Task Manager for Emacs. Proced is part of emacs 23.

Required modules:

  • Emacsvox-Proced Commands

15.186.1 Emacsvox-Proced Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-proced-jump-to-process
  • emacsvox-proced-next-field
  • emacsvox-proced-next-line
  • emacsvox-proced-previous-field
  • emacsvox-proced-previous-line
  • emacsvox-proced-speak-args
  • emacsvox-proced-speak-field
  • emacsvox-proced-speak-that-field
  • emacsvox-proced-speak-this-field

15.186.1.1 emacsvox-proced-jump-to-process ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-jump-to-process (name) ¶
Jump to process by name.

(fn NAME)

15.186.1.2 emacsvox-proced-next-field ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-next-field ¶
Navigate to next field.

15.186.1.3 emacsvox-proced-next-line ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-next-line ¶
Move to next line and speak a summary.

15.186.1.4 emacsvox-proced-previous-field ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-previous-field ¶
Navigate to previous field.

15.186.1.5 emacsvox-proced-previous-line ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-previous-line ¶
Move to next line and speak a summary.

15.186.1.6 emacsvox-proced-speak-args ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-speak-args ¶
Speak command  invocation  for this process.

15.186.1.7 emacsvox-proced-speak-field ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-speak-field (field-name) ¶
Speak value of specified field in current line.

(fn FIELD-NAME)

15.186.1.8 emacsvox-proced-speak-that-field ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-speak-that-field ¶
Speak desired field via single keystroke.

15.186.1.9 emacsvox-proced-speak-this-field ¶

Command: emacsvox-proced-speak-this-field (&optional position) ¶
Speak field at specified column — defaults to current column.

(fn &optional POSITION)
