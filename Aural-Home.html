6.3 Begin With Aural Home ¶

Open Aural Home from an ordinary buffer with C-e H. Emacsvox remembers that source buffer and opening position so contextual actions such as explanation and remapping operate on the object you were using. Moving the source cursor while Home is open does not change this target. In Home, C-c C-o returns to it. To choose a new target, invoke C-e H afresh from that item in an ordinary buffer.

Returning through Home or Help preserves the selected row and unfinished edits. Buffer rule editors retain separate drafts for each source buffer; reopening a modified editor resumes its draft. Reopening the same voice in the tuner likewise preserves its working settings. If a prepared remap’s source item changes or disappears, Emacsvox asks you to prepare that change again before applying it.

Aural Home begins with five task groups in one spoken table. Press RET or TAB to expand or collapse a group. Each action names a destination, reports its current status, and describes its purpose. / searches all actions, including collapsed groups; the matching group opens with your destination selected. The common controls are:

n, p, down, up

Move by row; settings lists speak the name and current state.

left, right

Move among the titled columns.

.

Speak the current column title and value.

SPC

Speak the complete current row.

RET

Open a destination, child list, or details. In the tuner, edit a value.

C-c C-a

Choose an applicable action by name.

N

Create a new item where supported.

P

Preview in managers and editors; Home retains its profiles shortcut.

S

Stop the preview and its remaining samples.

?

Display and speak help for the current Aural interface.

h

Return to Aural Home from a manager or editor.

q

Leave the current Aural interface.

The five groups are:

Task groupDestinations
Understand or change feedbackCurrent item explanation and remapping, Recent Feedback, semantic vocabulary, and return to source.
Choose voices and soundsBrowse and try voices, palettes, sound packs, speech engine and rate, notifications, spatial settings, and the full Voice Workbench.
Choose optional feedbackPresentation Options and training explanations.
Manage changes and saved setupsUnfinished drafts, overrides, buffer rules, and Presentation Profiles.
TroubleshootAural Doctor and Omnivox engine modules.

Home announces the captured source and unfinished draft count on entry. Choose Resume unfinished changes to visit the original rule editor, tuner, or routing Workbench. The draft is still unsaved until you accept it there. Presentation Profiles capture options, palette, sound pack, and spatial settings. Physical voice routes have their own saved routing profile.

You can press RET on a row without memorizing its shortcut. Aural Home also provides direct keys for frequent routes, including x for explanation, O for overrides, H for recent feedback, P for profiles, V for voice palettes, W for Voice Workbench, and D for Aural Doctor.
