6.5.1 Walkthrough: Make A Personal Reading Palette ¶

This example gives you a personal place to adjust standard voices. Use an unused palette name; reading-demo below is an example. Note your current palette before starting so you can return to it.

  1. Open C-e H V for Voice palettes. Press N, enter reading-demo, give it a purpose such as “My reading experiments”, and choose acss-default as its parent. Creation saves the new palette, but does not activate it. Its standard voices are inherited.
  2. Press RET to enter the new palette. Find bolden and move to its Source column. It should name acss-default. . repeats the column title and value; SPC reads the whole row. P previews the voice.
  3. Press e to edit bolden here. The editor identifies the destination palette and inherited source. Move to Average Pitch, press RET, and enter 6 (or 5 if it is already six). B compares the opening voice with the edited voice using the same text. Unsupported adjustments are reported; see Voice Adjustments if the contrast is unclear.
  4. Choose Save to collection without selecting. Wait for the save result, then use q to return to the voice list. Source should now identify reading-demo for bolden. Other inherited entries still name their providers. Saving has created a complete local entry, not a pitch-only patch on the parent.
  5. Press o to return to the palette manager and a on reading-demo to activate it. Check its status, then revisit content that uses bolden. This affects all uses resolving to your edited entry. Explanation at the source helps distinguish palette settings from additional contextual rules.
  6. If you want this palette after restart, press U on it in the palette manager and review the startup proposal. It updates the palette selection in the saved startup profile. If no startup profile exists, review the proposed new profile’s other settings too. Saving the palette and selecting it for startup are separate actions.
  7. To return to your previous sound, activate the original palette. If you also changed startup, use U on that original palette to restore the startup selection. Your saved reading-demo remains available for later experiments. To remove only its bolden customization, follow the Reset example in Inheritance, Aliases, and Reset.
