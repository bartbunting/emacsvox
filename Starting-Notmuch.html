10.1 Start Notmuch ¶

Run M-x notmuch to open Hello. With the standard Emacsvox global maps loaded, C-' m runs notmuch-search directly and asks for a query. Emacsvox loads its integration when Notmuch loads and marks new Hello, Search, Show, and Tree buffers with Notmuch semantic context.

Loading speech support does not alter mail, tags, files, or Notmuch configuration. Notmuch owns database, tag, archive, attachment, and composition operations; Emacsvox presents the result of the action you invoke.
