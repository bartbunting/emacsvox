8.1 Document Authoring ¶

Emacs major modes provide the editing commands for each document format. Emacsvox speaks ordinary movement and editing, applies voice-lock where the mode exposes useful faces, and adds mode-specific feedback where an integration exists. Open or create the document in its normal Emacs mode, then use C-h m to discover that mode’s workflow. For outlines and templates, see Structured Editing And Templates.
