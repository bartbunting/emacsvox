6.4.4 Review And Remove Your Rules ¶

In Your feedback rules, RET or e opens the selected rule’s settings panel. In the rule list, the same keys open that panel. Only the selected rule appears while its panel is open; the other rules remain in the working draft. n and p move through the visible settings; SPC reads the current setting and value. RET expands What should match, Content, Before content, or After content. Matching criteria toggle between included and excluded. Within Content, voice and speak expand their choices in place, showing the current choice. Other fields ask only for the selected value. Existing actions expand into their individual settings; Change operation offers the operation choices for that phase. Inherit removes the selected voice or speech override; D removes another selected setting. Each edit preserves the rule’s other settings and stays in the working draft.

P previews the working rule and S stops. w or the Save row saves to the original scope and announces whether the rule was saved or applied temporarily. q closes the current section and focuses its parent, retaining unfinished edits. For example, from a voice choice it returns to voice, then Content. At the top level it closes the panel and returns to the full rule list. From the rule list, N adds a rule and q asks before discarding an unfinished draft.

Open Your feedback rules to manage these changes together. There, P previews the complete current result, t temporarily disables or enables a rule, and d removes it. Disabling or deleting an override restores the next weaker behavior; it does not leave a copied default behind.
