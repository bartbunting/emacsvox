Emacsvox ¶

Welcome to the Emacsvox user manual. Begin with the introduction, follow the installation path until Emacsvox speaks, and then try the guided first session in Basic Usage. Platform and compatibility detail is available in Speech Backends when you need it. Exact commands, options, and keymaps are in the separate Emacsvox Reference Manual in Emacsvox Reference Manual. Selected historical Emacspeak material is in the separate Emacspeak Heritage in Emacspeak Heritage manual and is not current setup guidance. For rapidly evolving Omnivox and Aural Presentation details, also consult Readme.org and etc/aural-presentation-reference.org in the source tree.
