8.10.6 Inspect And Maintain A Personal Sound Pack ¶

In the manager, o opens the directory and g rescans. For a discovered personal pack, e edits summary, parent, coverage profiles, and default spatialisation. Built-ins are read-only. Editing metadata saves immediately; this is not a retained voice-editor draft.

Coverage profiles declare cue sets for validation, not working Presentation Profiles. Native assets belong to the pack; effective ones include inherited and fallback cues. Validation and details explain those sources.

This manager does not record/edit audio or create a sound pack through a New Palette-style wizard. New packs need audio files and resource metadata; see Authoring Sound Packs in the Developer Guide. For a change to when a cue plays, use the feedback-rule workflow.
