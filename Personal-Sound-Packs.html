6.8.5 Inspect And Maintain A Personal Sound Pack ¶

In the sound-pack manager, o opens the pack directory and g rescans and refreshes discovered resources. For a discovered personal pack, e offers guided metadata editing: summary, parent, coverage profiles, and default spatialization. Registered built-in packs are read-only in this editor. Editing metadata saves it; this is not an unsaved voice-style draft.

A pack’s coverage profiles declare sets of cues it intends to supply. They are validation requirements, not Presentation Profiles that select your working setup. Native assets belong to the pack; effective assets also include inherited and fallback cues. Validation and individual cue details explain that distinction.

The manager does not record or edit audio, and it has no New Palette-style sound-pack creator. Preparing a new pack requires its audio files and resource metadata. See Authoring Sound Packs in the Developer Guide; for an ordinary change to when a cue plays, use Change this feedback.
