15.242 emacsvox-which-key ¶

Which-Key displays available keybindings in a popup after pressing a prefix key. This module speech-enables Which-Key to provide auditory feedback for available bindings, supporting navigation through pages and speaking the current binding options.

  • Emacsvox-Which-Key Commands

15.242.1 Emacsvox-Which-Key Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-which-key-speak
  • emacsvox-which-key-toggle-auto-speak

15.242.1.1 emacsvox-which-key-speak ¶

Command: emacsvox-which-key-speak ¶
Speak the current or last which-key popup content.

15.242.1.2 emacsvox-which-key-toggle-auto-speak ¶

Command: emacsvox-which-key-toggle-auto-speak ¶
Toggle automatic speaking of which-key popups.
