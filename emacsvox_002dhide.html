15.142 emacsvox-hide ¶

Flexible hide and show for emacsvox. This module allows one to easily hide or expose blocks of lines starting with a common prefix. It is motivated by the need to flexibly hide quoted text in email but is designed to be more general. the prefix parsing is inspired by filladapt.el

  • Emacsvox-Hide Commands

15.142.1 Emacsvox-Hide Commands ¶

  • emacsvox-hide-or-expose-all-blocks
  • emacsvox-hide-or-expose-block
  • emacsvox-hide-speak-block-sans-prefix

15.142.1.1 emacsvox-hide-or-expose-all-blocks ¶

Command: emacsvox-hide-or-expose-all-blocks ¶
Hide or expose all blocks.

15.142.1.2 emacsvox-hide-or-expose-block ¶

Command: emacsvox-hide-or-expose-block (&optional prefix) ¶
C-e j ¶
Hide or expose a block of text.
 Optional interactive prefix arg causes all blocks in current
buffer to be hidden or exposed.

(fn &optional PREFIX)

15.142.1.3 emacsvox-hide-speak-block-sans-prefix ¶

Command: emacsvox-hide-speak-block-sans-prefix ¶
C-e C-j ¶
Speaks current block after stripping its prefix.
