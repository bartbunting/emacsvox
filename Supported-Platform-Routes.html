14.1.1 Version and capability requirements ¶

Omnivox 1.18.0 is the minimum supported release. It supplies named punctuation profiles, palette-aware character speech and editable engine configuration, alongside managed voice libraries, eSpeak variants, engine-specific voice controls, individual fallback tuning and complete voice previews. A newer compatible release may be used. The manual archive examples and guided installers pin the 1.18.0 core and matching optional engines exactly; the macOS Homebrew tap follows its maintained stable formula.

Updating Emacsvox source alone does not upgrade Omnivox. Rerunning a guided installer can install its pinned Omnivox release; Homebrew and manual installations use their own update procedures. Check which route owns the selected installation before changing it. Voice-library management uses the bundled local management route. Remote and direct-native launches do not acquire the WSL launcher’s file or release-management capabilities merely by connecting to an Omnivox worker. The individual procedures identify their required host and capabilities.
