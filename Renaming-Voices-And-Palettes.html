6.5.3 Rename Voices And Palettes ¶

Press r in the voice list to rename a custom voice defined directly in that personal palette. Enter the new name to save it; the renamed voice keeps its settings, physical choices and individual tuning. Save or discard unfinished voice edits first. Standard Emacsvox voice names cannot be renamed; copy one to create a custom voice. For an inherited voice, copy it or rename it in its owning palette. Renaming updates personal, session and buffer presentation rules, including face mappings, and loaded global and buffer face maps. Personal rules and the voice are saved together; a failed save leaves the old name and mappings usable. Face mappings declared in your own Lisp configuration must also be updated there for future sessions. References in maintained integration rules, Presentation Options, or other voice definitions still block the rename; update those uses first. Renaming also stops if another palette directly defines either name, because mappings are shared.

To rename a personal palette, select it in Voice Palettes, press r, and enter its new name. The rename is saved immediately. It preserves voice settings and individual fallback tuning, updates child palettes and personal Presentation Profiles, and keeps an active palette selected under its new name. Save or discard unfinished voice edits first. Built-in palettes cannot be renamed. A reference from maintained integration configuration can block a rename; its owner must update that reference first.
