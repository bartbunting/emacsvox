15.73 emacsvox-company ¶

COMPANY -mode: Complete Anything Support for emacs. This module provides an Emacsvox Company Front-end, And advises the needed interactive commands in Company. It adds an emacsvox-specific front-end emacsvox-company-frontend to the value of company-frontends. Note that company-frontends is a user-customizable option and ends up getting saved by emacs along with other custom settings. Function emacsvox-company-frontend handles providing spoken feedback, and leaves it to other frontends on company-frontends to generate their own feedback.
