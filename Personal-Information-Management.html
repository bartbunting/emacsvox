9.10 Personal Information Management ¶

Calendar, Org, and related packages define their data formats and workflows. Emacsvox presents navigation, state changes, and structured content when those modes are active. Begin with the application’s manual and live help, then consult its matching integration in the generated reference.

Org’s focused menu at C-e S includes its table-editing feedback choices. Those are separate from the shared reading mode above: the focused menu’s global default does not replace an existing buffer-local choice, and the shared reader retains its own title and speaking preferences.
