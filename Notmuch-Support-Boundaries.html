10.7 Know the support boundaries ¶

Hello

Widget navigation, opening or closing the view, and searches have Notmuch-specific feedback.

Search

Results, background search completion, tags, archiving, opening, and configured detail inspection have Notmuch-specific feedback.

Show

Messages, HTML data tables, MIME parts, pages, visibility, tags, archiving, and explicit inspection have Notmuch-specific feedback.

Tree

Ordinary reading works, and presentation rules can identify the Notmuch context. There are no specialised row summaries or feedback for Tree navigation, tag changes, folding or background updates.

Composition

Standard Message mode owns composition. Speech after new-mail, reply, forward, or resume transitions belongs to emacsvox-message, not the Notmuch integration.

Once loaded, Notmuch speech support and its Search arrow bindings stay active for the Emacs session. There is no separate unload or speech-disable command; loading the integration again does not add duplicate feedback.
