TABULATED-LIST == tabulated list mode Speech-enable tabulated lists and provide commands for intelligent spoken output

  • emacsvox-tabulated-list-next-row

15.223.1.1 emacsvox-tabulated-list-next-row ¶

Command: emacsvox-tabulated-list-next-row ¶
Move to next row and speak that cell

15.223.1.2 emacsvox-tabulated-list-previous-row ¶

Command: emacsvox-tabulated-list-previous-row ¶
Move to previous row and speak that cell.

15.223.1.3 emacsvox-tabulated-list-speak-cell ¶

Command: emacsvox-tabulated-list-speak-cell (&optional movement-icon) ¶
Speak the current cell with optional MOVEMENT-ICON.
